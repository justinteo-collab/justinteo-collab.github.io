# Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private assistant app used only by Justin Teo to connect his own Google account (Gmail, Calendar and Drive) to his personal assistant running on his own computer.

## Privacy policy

The app reads and acts on Justin's own Gmail, Calendar and Drive data only, and only when he asks it to. Data stays on his own computer and is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
